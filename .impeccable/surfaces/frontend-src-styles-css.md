# Surface brief: Vulcan UCF app shell and screens

Mode: Operate. Audience: technical security team (SOC and detection engineers, analysts); auditors read. Task: see what is outstanding, work through use cases, interviews and requests, in long daily sessions on desktop, sometimes dim rooms.
Chosen by the user: Dark Console, with earth-tone highlights instead of cold syntax blues. Not pinned in the brief beyond that.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm graphite working surface where hairline seams, not shadows or cards-in-cards, divide the work, and colour appears only to mean something. Refuses the cyber look: no neon, no glow, no scanlines, no terminal costume, no blue-black.
OWN-WORLD: Warm graphite grounds (not blue-black), text off-white with a slight warm cast. Earth accents: ember (primary action, active nav), ochre (warning, fair), moss (good), clay red (gap, danger), slate (info, focus). One-pixel seams between panels, one elevation step, radius 6. One grotesk (Inter) with tabular figures for all data, JetBrains Mono only for codes and IDs; Space Grotesk is dropped.
STORY: A team member opens the app and sees at once what needs attention, reads coverage as figures that line up, and acts. Destructive actions sit apart with space and stay outline until focused.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Dashboard: dark rail left (220 px) with ember active entry, header with title and two actions, a Needs-attention panel with big aligned counts and a button each, four key figures in a row with rating pills, the L1 detection table with three-bar metrics; seams not shadows.
FORM: Dark Console (challenger digital-design-canon-dark-first-developer-console, competitive), steered to earth accents. seed: seed:bc61b7d39a453c38
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
